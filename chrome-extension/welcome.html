⚠️ Honest limitations — what this tool cannot do

  • It classifies; it cannot block. It cannot stop a message, call or payment, freeze money, or file a report for you. If money was lost, call 1930, report at cybercrime.gov.in and inform your bank immediately.
  • A verdict is evidence, not a guarantee. “LOOKS SAFE” is not proof a message is safe, and “FRAUD” is not proof of a crime. On 64 genuinely unseen test messages the model got about 4 in 5 right — genuine bank-alert-style messages, especially in Telugu and Hindi, are its weakest spot. Verify alerts on your bank's official number.
  • Text only. It reads text you paste, select or drag. It cannot scan your SMS inbox, WhatsApp, calls, screenshots, QR codes, voice notes, or the page a link leads to.
  • Three languages only. Telugu, Hindi and English (including Hinglish and Romanized Telugu). Other languages and scripts are not supported yet.
  • New scams can slip through. Fraud patterns change daily; a pattern the model has never seen may score low until corrections retrain it. Scores near the threshold are shown as UNCERTAIN instead of a forced answer.
  • Training data is thinner than we want. Real Hindi and Telugu messages — genuine ones above all — are scarce in the training set, so real-world accuracy will differ from test numbers. Every figure we publish names its test basis.
  • Desktop Chromium only, and the first check is slow. The 239 MB model runs on your device; the first verdict can take ~30 seconds. It does not run inside mobile apps.
  • Not a government body. Not legal advice. Fraud Detector is an independent, student-built tool. The official routes are the 1930 helpline and cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Sharing is your choice — know what it sends. Corrections stay on your device unless you turn sharing ON in Settings; then only anonymized text (numbers, links and OTPs replaced by placeholders) is queued. Anonymization can miss unusual formats, so don't correct messages containing details you would not want shared anonymized.
